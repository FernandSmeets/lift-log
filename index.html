Privacy statement

Lift Log is made by Pixelplanners (Fernand Smeets). This statement explains what happens with your data when you use a Lift Log account.

What we store

  • Your email address and your password (stored only as a secure hash, never readable).
  • Your training data: exercises, templates, workouts, weights, reps and settings.
  • When you last logged in and the type of browser or phone you use, so you can stay logged in.
  • For failed login attempts: your IP address, for at most one day, to block password guessing.
  • If you use Weight: your weigh-ins and your goal weight.
  • If you use Buddies: the name you choose, who your buddies are, what you share with each of them and your emoji reactions.

Why

Only to give you an account, keep your training data safe and make it available on your other devices. We don't use your data for advertising, don't sell it and don't share it with others.

Buddies

Buddies only see something from you after you've added each other with a code. Per buddy, you choose what they see: only the days you trained, also which exercises, or also your weights and PRs. They see your chosen name, never your email address. You can pause sharing or remove a buddy at any time in Buddies.

Where

Your data is stored on servers of our hosting provider STRATO in Germany. Data is sent over an encrypted connection.

How long

As long as you have an account. You can delete your account and all its data yourself at any time in Settings → ••• → Account.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or take a copy (Settings → ••• → Data → export). Questions or requests: info@pixelplanners.nl. If you're not happy with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Delete account

This permanently deletes your account and all training data saved in it. This can't be undone. Enter your password to confirm.
